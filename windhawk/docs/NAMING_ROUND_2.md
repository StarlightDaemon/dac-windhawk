# Naming round 2 — beyond Liminal

Research and creative report — 2026-10-05.

The operator likes the atmosphere of **Liminal**, but requested alternatives
after finding an adjacent screensaver project. No replacement is selected or
applied in this round. Liminal OLED Guard remains the local RC4 artifact label;
it is not a settled public brand.

## Direction

Preserve the feeling of **a threshold between waking and rest, light and shadow,
or ordinary space and another realm**. Favor names that could belong to a quiet
shipboard subsystem or a place in speculative fiction, while sounding natural
in a Windows tray menu. Avoid relying on a recognizable franchise character.

Keep function in the descriptor:

> **[Name] OLED Guard**
> Activity-aware OLED protection and per-monitor screensavers.

The purpose remains reducing prolonged static-image exposure, not guaranteeing
burn-in prevention. The OLED descriptor reflects the operator's chosen focus;
it does not mean the existing multi-monitor features cease working on LCDs.

## Vocabulary map

These are semantic directions, not claims that the bare words are available.

| Direction | Useful words/images | Fit and caution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Between states | Threshold, interstice, passage, veil | Closest to Liminal's appeal; avoid generic portal/security branding |
| Twilight | Gloaming, vespertine, dusk, penumbra | Natural bridge from a lit display into rest; many established uses |
| Near sleep | Hypnagogic, oneiric, reverie | Suggests dreamlike presentation; keep clear of human sleep/medical claims |
| Quiet protection | Shade, ward, shelter, stillness | Connects to black blanking without promising repair or locking |
| Cosmic transition | Eclipse, terminator, event horizon | Strong sci-fi imagery, but crowded vocabulary and often awkward utility names |

For lexical grounding, Merriam-Webster describes the
[hypnagogic period](https://www.merriam-webster.com/wordplay/more-useful-obscure-words-youve-probably-never-used/slugabed)
as the approach to sleep, and groups gloaming with
[twilight/evening](https://www.merriam-webster.com/thesaurus/TWILIGHT?dir=z&file=zhuzh&lang=en_us&pronunciation=).
The invented names below use those associations; invented spellings are not
presented as genuine Latin words or established dictionary definitions.

## Three directions to develop

### Betweenlight OLED Guard

Best match to the *meaning* that made Liminal appealing. It suggests a boundary
state rather than merely night or darkness. The name is comprehensible without
knowledge of a fictional universe.

- Tray: **Betweenlight — watching idle**
- Window: **Betweenlight OLED Guard Settings**
- Action: **Pause Betweenlight**
- Visual direction: a narrow violet horizon separating a lit panel from black.

Main weakness: the full title is relatively long. Use Betweenlight in compact
surfaces; keep OLED Guard in the mod listing, window title and release material.

### Hypnial OLED Guard

Closest to a compact, standalone system name. It evokes the moment before sleep
without reusing Liminal or appending a near-identical suffix to it. This is an
invented brand candidate, not a claim about a real sleep state.

- Tray: **Hypnial — watching idle**
- Window: **Hypnial OLED Guard Settings**
- Action: **Pause Hypnial**
- Visual direction: a softly closing aperture or interrupted orbital ring.

Main weakness: pronunciation and spelling require testing aloud. Do not choose
it solely because fewer search results surfaced. Its health/sleep association
also makes the OLED descriptor important.

### Vesperveil OLED Guard

Strongest overt fantasy atmosphere. More evocative of a protective enchantment
than a utility command. It fits the existing violet visual language naturally.

- Tray: **Vesperveil — watching idle**
- Window: **Vesperveil OLED Guard Settings**
- Action: **Pause Vesperveil**
- Visual direction: a thin veil crossing a dark display, with a small evening star.

Main weakness: existing non-software uses make it a less distinct discovery
candidate. [Frost Pen Co. sells a Vesperveil pen](https://frostpenco.com/collections/sealed-archive-collection/products/vesperveil-andrea-bespoke-fountain-pen),
and [a music release uses Vesperveil Records](https://open.spotify.com/album/5lnYKyCOXyZ8mgK1CHo8OC).
Those are factual collisions, not a conclusion about whether software use is permitted.

## Recommendation and next decision

Develop **Betweenlight**, **Hypnial**, and **Vesperveil** visually and verbally
before selecting one. My preference is **Betweenlight OLED Guard** for carrying
Liminal's meaning forward; Hypnial is the compact sci-fi alternative, and
Vesperveil is the fantasy alternative.

For the chosen finalist, check spaced/hyphenated spellings, relevant software
catalogs, handles and intended release markets before public adoption. Try the
name aloud and in the actual tray/settings UI. Preserve RC4's technical identity
and recovery compatibility through any future rename; another visible branding
change should not create another enabled controller or move fault state.

No source, version, installation or release archive was changed by this report.


## Follow-up: OLEDGuard conditional selection — 2026-10-05

The operator authorized OLEDGuard as the canonical name only if research found
no strong red flag. Exact searches for OLEDGuard, OLED Guard and OLED-Guard found
an existing product directly in our market:

- [OLED Guard Pro official website](https://oledguard.com/) markets a Windows
  OLED-protection utility with per-display overlays and DDC/CI controls; it owns
  the exact oledguard.com domain.
- [Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4558060/OLED_Guard_Pro/)
  confirms the product, developer QPRO, publisher SQNco and September 16, 2026
  release date.
- [OLED Guard Chrome extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/oled-guard-%E2%80%94-screen-dimme/oppliolimnnddlmblnnhabgbhgjpabfg)
  is another OLED-protection use of the same words, published by Rooverr
  Technologies Inc.

Disposition: this meets the operator's red-flag condition. Same name, purpose,
Windows audience and domain create substantial practical confusion; removing
spaces does not meaningfully distinguish the product. No trademark determination
is needed to reject this candidate on product-identification grounds. The
conditional rename was not applied; RC4 and its existing label remain unchanged.
This also weakens OLED Guard as a distinctive descriptor for any future brand.

## Follow-up: DRIFT and IDLE conflict screen — 2026-10-06

The operator requested screening of the current acronym candidates, including
even loosely related uses:

- DRIFT: Display Rest and Idle Function Toolkit.
- IDLE: Independent Display Lifecycle Engine.

Recommendation: reject both standalone names under that threshold. The full
expansions produced no relevant exact match in the searches reviewed, but that
does not distinguish the short names people would see, speak and search for.
Capitalization likewise does not resolve practical confusion.

### DRIFT

- [phlx0/drift](https://github.com/phlx0/drift) is an existing terminal
  screensaver. Its documented shell integration activates on inactivity and
  returns to the prompt on input. It offers animated scenes and themes, with
  Windows binaries as well as macOS/Linux builds. Windows lacks its automatic
  shell integration. This is an exact name in the screensaver category, though
  not an independent monitor power/protection controller like our mod.
- [Drift Clock](https://github.com/AnshTandon05/drift-clock) is a Windows 10/11
  clock screensaver with animated backgrounds and input dismissal. Its compound
  name and desktop screensaver purpose are further nearby use.

The exact screensaver name is sufficient to fail this screen without relying
on unrelated software brands or determining trademark status.

### IDLE

- [idle by Kamina.io](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.idle.app&hl=en)
  is an Android bedside-clock app explicitly marketed around OLED protection,
  pixel shifting and adaptive brightness. Exact short name and overlapping
  screen-care positioning; different platform and activation model. Its claims
  of completely preventing burn-in are marketing claims, not verified findings
  of this review.
- [IdleDimmer](https://github.com/UnDadFeated/IdleDimmer) is a Windows per-monitor
  dimming utility with independent display controls, idle-triggered overlays up
  to black, and OLED-protection positioning. This is a compound-name overlap
  with particularly close functionality. Its documented idle trigger uses
  system keyboard/mouse inactivity; independent dim controls should not be
  mistaken for our per-monitor activity attribution.
- [IdleScreen / Idle Overlay](https://github.com/EatKFCinMc/IdleScreen) is a
  Windows app that shows a fullscreen black overlay after a configurable idle
  interval and explicitly targets OLED use.
- [IdleStyle](https://github.com/Jax-Core/IdleStyle) and
  [Idle Webview](https://github.com/EnhancedJax/IdleWebview) are Windows
  screensaver projects with multiple-display support. These are further nearby
  compound names rather than exact IDLE matches.
- [Python IDLE](https://docs.python.org/3/library/idle.html) already uses the
  exact uppercase name for its editor and shell. It is functionally unrelated
  but adds a substantial software identity/search collision.

### Scope and disposition

Checked public web results for both names with screensaver, screen saver,
monitor, display, app, OLED and GitHub qualifiers, and both quoted full acronym
expansions. Opened the primary project pages, Python documentation and app-store
listing for the decisive findings. This is a practical naming screen, not a
complete catalog search or trademark clearance. No absence of other uses is
claimed. Existing exact and adjacent uses already answer the operator's test.

Both candidates remain unselected; recommendation is to retire them from the
standalone-name shortlist. No branding, source, build, installation or archive
was changed. Future candidates should be screened before presenting them as
viable finalists; a unique acronym expansion alone is insufficient.
